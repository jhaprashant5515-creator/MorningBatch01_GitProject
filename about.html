About Prashant Sir

Prashant Sir is an inspiring trainer and mentor who teaches Spring Boot and Microservices at NareshIT. He is widely appreciated for making difficult backend concepts easy to understand through clear explanations and practical examples.

His teaching focuses on real-world enterprise application development, helping students understand how modern Java-based systems are built, deployed, and maintained in professional environments.

He guides learners through important topics such as:

  • Spring Boot fundamentals
  • REST APIs and web services
  • Spring MVC and dependency injection
  • Microservices architecture
  • Service communication and integration
  • Best practices for scalable applications
